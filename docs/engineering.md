# Engineering content and PDF/E source profiles

`PdfEngineering.Inspect` passively inventories every active object/direct value, including unattached U3D/PRC/other3D streams,3D annotation dictionaries, rich media, geospatial dictionaries and embedded-file payloads. Records carry physical object/key provenance and exact encoded stream SHA256 where applicable. Streams are hashed without decoding or invoking media engines, scripts, native viewers or geospatial interpreters. This is preserve-only inspection; no advanced-content renderer is bundled. Zero records is not a conformance result.

`Preserve` stages a bounded nonempty PDF and copies its exact original bytes. Matching SHA256/length proves all source associations, opaque payloads, revision history and signature bytes remain identical; it does not establish signature validity or PDF/E conformance. Streams remain caller-owned and must be distinct.

`PdfProfileConversions` also accepts the exact PDF/E-1:2008 edition with an explicit producer and separately identified complete independent validator. Input/output/profile/settings digests and retained page geometry are checked under the shared context. No unused print-intent setting is accepted for this engineering gate. For advanced source content, the managed gate proves retention only when complete file bytes remain identical. A different encoding/rewrite requires a future qualified semantic-preservation proof or explicit `AllowAdvancedContentLoss` plus permissive policy; a stable loss diagnostic records that associations/semantics are not proven retained. Ordinary parsing never executes payloads.

Source tests use original opaque data to prove inventory/provenance/byte retention and gate behavior. Their payloads are explicitly not genuine U3D/PRC corpora, and their validator fixtures test binding only. Full PDF/E generation/conversion, complete independent validators, semantic preservation across rewrites,3D/rich-media/geospatial/attachment real-format corpora and actual supported-host/package production qualification remain mandatory for FP-069, which stays Planned.
